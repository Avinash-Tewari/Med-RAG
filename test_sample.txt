CLINICAL GUIDELINES: MANAGEMENT OF TYPE 2 DIABETES MELLITUS

1. DIAGNOSTIC CRITERIA
Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (T2DM) is diagnosed when any of the following criteria are met:
- Fasting plasma glucose (FPG) >= 126 mg/dL (7.0 mmol/L)
- 2-hour plasma glucose >= 200 mg/dL (11.1 mmol/L) during an oral glucose tolerance test (OGTT)
- HbA1c >= 6.5% (48 mmol/mol)
- Random plasma glucose >= 200 mg/dL (11.1 mmol/L) in a patient with classic symptoms of hyperglycemia

2. FIRST-LINE TREATMENT
Metformin is recommended as the first-line pharmacological therapy for T2DM:
- Starting dose: 500 mg once or twice daily with meals
- Maximum dose: 2000-2550 mg/day in divided doses
- Contraindicated in patients with eGFR < 30 mL/min/1.73m2
- Should be temporarily discontinued before iodinated contrast procedures
- Common side effects: gastrointestinal symptoms (nausea, diarrhea, abdominal pain)
- Reduces HbA1c by approximately 1.0-1.5%

3. SECOND-LINE THERAPY OPTIONS
If HbA1c target is not achieved with metformin monotherapy after 3-6 months, consider adding:
- SGLT2 inhibitors (empagliflozin, dapagliflozin): Preferred in patients with heart failure or CKD
- GLP-1 receptor agonists (liraglutide, semaglutide): Preferred in patients with established ASCVD
- DPP-4 inhibitors (sitagliptin, linagliptin): When cost is a concern or patient prefers oral therapy
- Sulfonylureas (glimepiride, gliclazide): Cost-effective but higher hypoglycemia risk
- Insulin therapy: When HbA1c > 10% or symptomatic hyperglycemia

4. MONITORING SCHEDULE
- HbA1c: Every 3 months until at target, then every 6 months
- Fasting lipid profile: Annually
- Serum creatinine and eGFR: Annually (more frequent if CKD present)
- Urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (UACR): Annually
- Comprehensive foot exam: Annually
- Dilated eye exam: Annually or as recommended by ophthalmologist
- Blood pressure: Every visit, target < 130/80 mmHg

5. LIFESTYLE MODIFICATIONS
- Medical nutrition therapy: Individualized meal planning with a registered dietitian
- Physical activity: At least 150 minutes/week of moderate-intensity aerobic exercise
- Weight management: 5-7% weight loss for overweight/obese patients
- Smoking cessation: Strongly recommended for all patients
- Alcohol: Limit to moderate consumption (1 drink/day for women, 2 for men)

6. CARDIOVASCULAR RISK MANAGEMENT
- Statin therapy: Moderate-intensity for all T2DM patients aged 40-75 years
- Aspirin therapy: Consider low-dose (75-162 mg/day) for secondary prevention
- Blood pressure management: ACE inhibitors or ARBs preferred, especially with albuminuria
- Target LDL cholesterol: < 100 mg/dL (< 70 mg/dL if established ASCVD)
